amcu automation window

An agent drives the other tabs of this window in the background. It never takes your focus; you can minimise this window or leave it behind your own. Closing it ends the agent's browser work.

这是 amcu 的自动化窗口:agent 在这个窗口的其他标签页里后台工作, 不会抢占你的焦点。窗口可以最小化或压在其他窗口后面;关闭它会中断 agent 正在进行的浏览器操作。
